HVSB — title/abstract trainer

Didactic cheat-sheet. One record → Yes / No / Unsure → verdict → Next record clears and starts at H.

What the hooks mean

H — Humans
Human participants, any age. Animals / synthetic voice / model-only = No.
V — Voice measured
Acoustics (F0, jitter, MFCC…) or judges scoring that talk: speech disturbances, fluency, pauses, verbal content, “how they sounded”. That is still V — Hollandsworth 1978 style. TTS / someone else’s avatar / they only spoke = No. Title says speech anxiety but only a questionnaire, nobody coded the speech = No. EGG, laryngeal EMG, neck accelerometer = V, not B.
S — State
Yes if a psychological state is induced (TSST, judges, threat) or measured (anxiety “now”, arousal, challenge/threat, mood, emotion, performance pressure; happiness counts if it is the emotion in that study).
No if: trait/diagnosis only; actors portray emotion; only exercise, sleep loss, pain, alcohol, noise; “stress” = syllable stress; competence (fluency, English, stuttering counts) with no state measure; bodily / muscle relaxation as a trained skill or treatment effect without a measured mood, anxiety, or stress score. Do not guess: “they gave a speech, so probably threat.”
B — Body
HR, HRV, ECG, ICG/CO/TPR/PEP, EDA/GSR, cortisol, EMG (not larynx), EEG, amylase — same people, same study. A questionnaire is not a body signal.
X5
Review, meta-analysis, editorial, protocol / registry with no results → Exclude + X5. Chase citations outside the PRISMA flow.
X6
Can you read the title/abstract in English? Yes = continue. No = Exclude + X6. Paywall ≠ other language. Sage / APA / Springer in English = Yes.
Maybe
Click Unsure when a hook is foggy (no n, unclear if they recorded the body, title in brackets and no methods). No hard No + at least one Unsure = Maybe. That is the trainer matching Rayyan.
Not a gate
Biofeedback / closed loop = FEEDBACK tag. ICG and verbal+vocal = charting / PhD hypothesis, not a hook.
