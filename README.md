# Voice × body × state — title/abstract rules

One text for both reviewers. Verification set, 5 October 2026. Rayyan project: **Voice-body verification set** (312 records).

This page is the rule. Do not use an older version.

## What you do

Screen title and abstract only. Click Include, Exclude, or Maybe.

- Do not open PDFs.
- Do not discuss individual papers until both reviewers finish.
- Blinding stays on until then.

## Question

How is a person's own voice measured together with their body's signals to read an internal state?

## Four gates — HVSB

All four are required.

**H — Humans.** Any age. Animals only, or no participants, fails H.

**V — Voice measured.** The person's own voice is measured: acoustics (pitch, intensity, rate, jitter, shimmer, MFCCs, vocalization intensity) or raters scoring that person's speech (fluency, disturbances, verbal content of their production).

V fails if the voice is only a stimulus, a playback, a chatbot, TTS, or the audience. Altered playback with no measure of the participant's voice fails V. Measures of the voice organ (electroglottography, laryngeal EMG, neck accelerometer, vocal airflow) count as V, not as body.

**S — State in the moment.** A psychological state is induced or measured in that study: stress, anxiety, fear, emotion, mood, arousal, challenge/threat, or performance pressure.

S fails if emotion is only acted or portrayed. S fails if only a diagnosis or trait is compared and no state is measured in the moment. S fails if the state is only physical (exercise, fatigue, sleep loss, pain, alcohol, drugs, noise) with no psychological state. A relaxation skill, a training outcome, or a competence score is not S by itself.

**B — Body signal.** At least one physiological signal from the same participants in the same study: heart rate, HRV, ECG, ICG, cardiac output, TPR, PEP, EDA/GSR, cortisol, non-laryngeal EMG, EEG, amylase, or an equivalent.

Body pose, Kinect, or a camera of movement is not B. A questionnaire alone is not B. Voice-organ measures are V, not B. Same-clock recording is charted later. It is not required at this screen.

## Decision

- **Include** — H, V, S, and B are all met.
- **Exclude** — any one gate clearly fails.
- **Maybe** — none clearly fails, and at least one is unclear from the title and abstract.

If you would need the PDF to decide, click Maybe.

## Exclude codes (still click Exclude)

- **X5** — no primary data: review, meta-analysis, commentary, editorial, protocol, or registry entry without results.
- **X6** — not in English.

## Not a gate

Do not include or exclude for these. They are tags for later:

- feedback or a closed loop
- recorded on the same clock
- challenge/threat physiology specifically
- a self-report of the state

A paper can pass HVSB with no feedback. Feedback alone does not pass HVSB.

## Agreement

Cohen's κ is computed on Exclude versus not-Exclude (Include and Maybe pooled), after blinding is turned off.
